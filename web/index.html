    A FIELD GUIDE TO ESUSU

    One circle.
    Everyone’s turn.

    Esusu. Ajo. A simple tradition: we contribute together, and take turns receiving the pot.

    1. 01

      Choose your place

      Pick a payout slot. Earlier turns require more collateral.

    2. 02

      Keep the commitment

      Contribute each round yourself, or use an agent within your limits.

    3. 03

      Take your turn

      Receive the pot when your slot comes around.

    Missed dues use collateral. A default means a strike; depleted collateral can reduce later pots.
