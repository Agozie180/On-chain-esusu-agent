# Grant and sponsor brief

**Project:** On-chain Esusu-agent. Trust-minimized savings circles plus a spend-capped AI agent, for Portaldot V3 (EVM).
**Maintainer:** Agozie180 (solo builder, Nigeria). **License:** MIT. **Repo:** github.com/Agozie180/On-chain-Esusu-agent

## Status, honestly
Built and tested locally (101 contract/agent tests, 39 browser checks). Not yet deployed to Portaldot testnet, not audited, no users. A grant would fund the step from hackathon prototype to audited pilot.

## Why it fits an ecosystem fund
- Public goods already in the repo: a verified `0x` to `5...` address helper, a deploy kit and guide, a reusable reputation registry, and a bounded-agent vault pattern with HTTP 402 payments.
- A concrete, local use case with a sourced market (see [BUSINESS](BUSINESS.md)).
